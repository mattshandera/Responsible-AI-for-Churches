AI marks a fundamental shift in how humans interact both with technology and with one another. As with all major technological advancements before it, AI has the capability of spreading hope and good news of the Gospel to ever-widening groups of people. 

Beginning with the construction of the first major roads during the Roman Empire, technology has enabled the message of the Gospel to go out to all the world bearing fruit and changing lives. Like the Gutenberg Press, AI will allow for unprecedented access and proliferation of the Bible by:
* Improving access to the Bible and related content.
* Facilitating faster translation into new languages.
* Unlocking new modalities for interaction with God’s word.
* Discovering a myriad of yet-to-be-discovered paradigms for interaction.

As with the advent of the Internet, we acknowledge it isn’t a question of "if" AI will impact the world, but "how." AI, like roads, movable type, or the Internet, can be harnessed for great good or great harm. These principles endeavor to reflect our considerate use of this new technology and its capacity to share in, not supplant, the transformative work of Jesus Christ.

## The Principles

1. **Human-Centered:** We believe AI should be used to augment human creativity and connection, not replace the unique, God-given essence of human ministry.
2. **Transparency:** We will be open about when and how AI is used in our communications and operations.
3. **Accuracy & Truth:** We will verify AI-generated content to ensure it aligns with scriptural truth and factual reality.
4. **Bias Mitigation:** We recognize that AI can reflect human biases; we will actively work to identify and mitigate these to ensure all are treated with dignity.
5. **Privacy & Data Ethics:** We will protect the data of our congregants and staff with the highest level of integrity.
6. **Accountability:** Humans remain responsible for the outcomes and impact of AI-driven tools and decisions.
7. **Inclusivity:** AI should be used to make the Gospel more accessible to all, regardless of language, ability, or background.
8. **Stewardship:** We will evaluate the cost and efficiency of AI tools to ensure they are a wise use of the resources entrusted to the church.
9. **Empowerment:** We will provide training and resources to help our community navigate the AI era with wisdom.
10. **Human Oversight:** Critical decisions, especially those involving pastoral care or theological interpretation, must always involve human discernment.
11. **Safety:** We will prioritize tools that have robust safety measures to prevent the generation of harmful or inappropriate content.
12. **Collaboration:** We will share our learnings and best practices with the broader Body of Christ.
13. **Innovation for Good:** We will seek out ways AI can solve complex problems and meet the needs of the vulnerable.
14. **Theological Reflection:** We will maintain an ongoing dialogue about the intersection of faith, technology, and what it means to be made in the Image of God.
15. **Continuous Improvement:** These principles will evolve as technology and our understanding of its impact grow.
16. **Digital Provenance:** We will strive to maintain the integrity of our brand and voice. In an era of deepfakes and subversive technology, we will explore digitally signing content to defend our authenticity.
17. **Legal Compliance:** We will stay informed and compliant with evolving state and federal legal requirements regarding AI.
18. **Environmental Stewardship:** As believers committed to stewarding the Earth, we will consider and work to mitigate the environmental impact of the AI technologies we utilize.

## How We Use AI Today

While we are constantly experimenting with AI technologies to drive efficiency and performance across all church functions, our primary use cases today are in the generative AI space for marketing—specifically content creation for our blog, podcast, webinars, digital ads, and long-form content (e.g., reports, blueprints) and research.

We use a collection of Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) products for:
* **Speech-to-text transcription:** Converting sermons and meetings into written records.
* **Content summarization:** Creating highlights and key takeaways from long-form teaching.
* **Outline generation:** Brainstorming structures for lessons and series.
* **Image & video editing & generation:** Enhancing our existing outreach and brand visuals.
* **Copywriting:** Drafting initial social media posts and newsletters.
* **Research:** Gathering data and summarizing complex topics for further review.

### Operational Efficiency & Automation
We are also beginning to integrate AI agents to automate administrative and systems-level tasks, ensuring that all such automation is executed within strict human-defined parameters and subject to continuous review as outlined in **Principle 15**. This allows staff to be redirected toward high-relational ministry, as committed in **Principle 13**.

Our workflow automation and agentic AI focus includes:
* **Automated file organization:** Archival and management in cloud storage.
* **Data synchronization:** Transferring information between ministry platforms.
* **Routine report generation:** Distribution of standard metrics and updates.
* **Scheduled maintenance:** Regular system health checks and technical oversight.
