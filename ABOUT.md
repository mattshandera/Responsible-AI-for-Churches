# About This Document

## Origin and Inspiration
This document was inspired initially from Paul Roetzer, Founder & CEO, Marketing AI Institute in his blog [Responsible AI Manifesto for Marketing and Business (v1)](https://www.marketingaiinstitute.com/blog/responsible-ai-manifesto-for-marketing-and-business). Like Paul and the SmarterX organization before us, we invite you to use these principles under a [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) license as a starting point for your own responsible AI policies and practices. This Creative Commons license lets others remix, adapt, and build upon the work, even for commercial purposes, as long as they credit the source and license their new creations under the identical terms.
As the "AI revolution" began to reshape the landscape of communication and technology, it became clear that the Church needed a specific, theology-driven framework to navigate these changes. This document was created to bridge the gap between cutting-edge technology and the timeless mission of the Gospel.

## Our Philosophy
We view AI as a "new road"—a technological infrastructure that, much like the Roman roads of the first century or the Gutenberg Press of the 15th century, has the potential to accelerate the spread of God’s Word. However, infrastructure is neutral; it is the *stewardship* of that infrastructure that determines its impact.

Our approach is rooted in three core convictions:
1. **Theological Integrity:** AI should never replace the human element of pastoral care or the unique "voice" of the Church.
2. **Ethical Transparency:** We believe that being "as shrewd as snakes and as innocent as doves" requires total honesty about how we use these tools.
3. **Global Accessibility:** We are excited by AI's ability to break down language barriers and make the Bible accessible to every tribe, tongue, and nation.
